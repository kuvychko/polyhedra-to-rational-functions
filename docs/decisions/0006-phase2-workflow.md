# 0006: Phase 2 workflow: two parallel tracks joined by one catalog

Date: 2026-10-03. Status: accepted by the owner.

## Context

Phase 1 is complete (decision 0005). Phase 2 (`PROGRAM.md` §14–15) has two kinds of work with
different owners and paces:

- the website, which Claude builds and the owner reviews;
- the physical pieces, where Claude screens and exports, and the owner prints, photographs and
  uploads to Printables.

Four baseline pieces were already printed before Phase 2 started: the cube–octahedron dual, the
dodecahedron–icosahedron dual (130 mm), the icosidodecahedral star (130 mm) and the icosahedral
crown (130 mm).

## Decisions

1. **Two parallel tracks.** Track A is the website; track B covers screening, printing,
   photography and Printables (`PHASE2.md`).
2. **A single catalog is the interface between the tracks.** One file, `catalog/pieces.yaml`,
   with one entry per object. It holds:
   - the recipe and solid;
   - the status (`candidate → screened → meshed → printed → photographed → listed`);
   - the printed size;
   - the STL hashes;
   - the photo paths;
   - the Printables URL.

   The site's gallery and the print/upload checklist are both generated from it, so they cannot
   drift apart. The owner's track only edits catalog fields.
3. **Printables: one listing per piece, each offering both sizes.** Each listing has the 80 mm and
   130 mm STLs, tip to tip. The pieces are grouped in a Printables collection, and the site links
   each piece to its own listing. Media and meshes are CC BY 4.0 (decision 0001), and each listing
   states it.
4. **Screen every candidate at both sizes, and choose the printed size per piece.** The screening
   sheet reports, at 80 and 130 mm:
   - extent, waist (minimum radius) and volume;
   - mesh closure;
   - the closest zero–pole separation in millimetres, the "spike next to a pit" risk.
5. **Printed pieces keep their printed files.** For the four baseline prints, and any baseline
   piece printed later, Printables gets the exact STLs from figures_repo @ `292bec4`, with their
   hashes recorded in the catalog. They are not regenerated by the new pipeline, which differs by
   the exact normalization (0.55% on the cube–octahedron dual), so the files match the photos.
   New pieces use the new pipeline.
6. **Build locally, then release in one step.** GitHub Pages serves only public repositories on
   the free plan. CI builds the site strictly on every push, and review happens on local builds.
   Flipping the repository public and running the (manual) deploy workflow is one explicit owner
   action at release.

## Consequences

- STLs stay out of git, as before. The catalog records their hashes, and a script regenerates the
  new ones.
- Photos are the owner's and are committed under the media licence (CC BY 4.0), downscaled for
  the site.
- Printables URLs exist only after upload. The site shows "print files coming" until then, so
  the site can be finished before every piece is listed.
